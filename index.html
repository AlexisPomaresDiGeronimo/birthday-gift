For Danai

A LETTER WRITTEN WITH THE SEA STILL IN MY HEART

There are meetings that disappear with the sunset… and there are meetings that stay quietly inside you, long after the island, the music, and the waves are gone.

Meeting you in Paxos felt like one of those rare moments that time slows down for. The sea looked softer, the nights warmer, and every conversation with you felt strangely familiar, as if life had already written our paths somewhere before we ever arrived there.

Since then, I kept thinking about your smile, your eyes, and the feeling of walking beside you under the island sky. Some people become memories. You became a reason to travel.

And now, on the 24th of May, I will cross countries and seas again — this time to see you in Faro. Not because I had to. Because some connections deserve distance to be defeated.

Maybe life is made of these unexpected moments: two people meeting on a Greek island, and continuing the story under the Portuguese sun.

Until Faro… keep a little space for me in your thoughts, as I already keep one for you in mine.

Yours,
The boy you met in Paxos
